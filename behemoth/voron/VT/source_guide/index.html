    Warning

    Before ordering live idlers for your 3D printer, confirm whether your build uses 9 mm or 12 mm belts. The live idlers are press-fitted and difficult to replace, so choosing the correct size is important. If you order the wrong version, you will need to purchase the correct one.

    SET14 and SET15 are designed for 9 mm belts.

    SET16 and SET17 are designed for 12 mm belts.

    Tip

    The behemoth Toolhead is specifically designed for use with the mammoth-3D Hell Fire Hotend. However, it can also support other hotends when used with the universal adapter.

    Please review the following options carefully before placing your order:

    Set 25: Designed exclusively for the Hell Fire Hotend and is not compatible with other hotends.

    Set 26: Includes the universal hotend plate, allowing compatibility with a range of hotends such as the Rapido, Goliath Air, Goliath Water, and Tricorn hotends.

    Note: The Chube Hotend is currently not supported.
